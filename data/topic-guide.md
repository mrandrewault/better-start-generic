# Meanwhile Topic Guide

Written by Andrew. This is the guidebook the AI story check reads when it sorts
stories into topics. Each topic is roughly 5% of the magazine (one story in
every 20). "NOT" items are things to leave out of that topic.

The Meanwhile rules (no politics, violence, tragedy, outrage, and so on) always
come first. This guide never overrides them.

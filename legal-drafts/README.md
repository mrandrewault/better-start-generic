# MEANWHILE legal and editorial copy — working drafts

These drafts are written for the current MEANWHILE product as of August 28,
2026. They are working language for review and customization, not legal advice.

Before publication, replace every bracketed item and have counsel confirm:

- the operating entity and mailing address;
- the governing state and courts;
- the support, privacy and copyright email addresses;
- the hosting, authentication, email and analytics vendors actually used in
  production;
- the retention schedule and account-deletion procedure;
- whether the service is intended for users under 18;
- whether any advertising, analytics, cookies or newsletter product has been
  added; and
- whether state, national or international privacy addenda are required.

The account and newsletter choices must remain separate. Creating an account
should not enroll a person in marketing email. Store newsletter consent with a
timestamp, the language shown at consent and its source. Every marketing email
must contain a working unsubscribe link and the sender’s valid postal address.

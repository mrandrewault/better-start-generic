## 1. Information we collect

### Information you provide

- **Account information.** If you create an account, we collect your email
  address and an internal user identifier. MEANWHILE currently uses
  passwordless email links rather than a password you create for us.
- **Edition preferences.** We may store the topics, names, places, edition name
  and free-form interests you choose while making or tuning an edition.
- **Saved stories and feedback.** We may store stories you save, signals such as
  “More like this,” “Less,” “Too political” or “Too depressing,” and related
  timestamps.
- **Reading and delivery history.** We may store identifiers for stories and
  media delivered to you or shown in your edition. We use this primarily to
  prevent repeats and maintain a fresh feed.
- **Communications.** We collect the contents of messages you send us, together
  with the contact information needed to respond.
- **Email choices.** If we offer newsletters or product updates, we will store
  whether you separately chose to receive them, when you made that choice and
  when you changed or withdrew it.

### Information stored on your device

MEANWHILE uses browser local storage and session storage for functional
purposes, including your theme, edition preferences, saved stories, story
history, delivered-story ledger, feed snapshot, feedback, display state and
temporary notices. Some of this information may remain only in that browser
unless you sign in and choose to sync supported information to your account.

### Information collected automatically

Our hosting, security and authentication providers may process customary
technical information such as IP address, browser and device type, timestamps,
referring pages, requested URLs, errors and security events. We use this
information to operate, protect and diagnose the Service.

MEANWHILE does not currently request your device’s precise GPS location. If a
future local-discovery feature requests precise location, we will ask first and
update this Policy before using it.

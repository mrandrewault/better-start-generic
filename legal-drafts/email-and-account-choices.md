# Email and Account Choices

Your MEANWHILE account and the MEANWHILE newsletter are separate choices.

## Your account

An account lets your edition preferences, saved stories and supported reading
history follow you between devices. MEANWHILE uses a secure email sign-in link,
so there is no password to remember.

Creating an account does **not** automatically subscribe you to promotional or
newsletter email. We will still send necessary account messages, such as a
sign-in link, confirmation, security notice or response to a request you made.

## Optional newsletter

If you choose to subscribe, we may occasionally send you a MEANWHILE edition,
interesting finds, product news or requests for feedback. Every newsletter will
include an unsubscribe link.

You can stop newsletters without deleting your account. You can also delete
your account without being forced through a newsletter-unsubscribe process.

## Suggested signup language

### Account form

**Keep your edition wherever you go.**  
Enter your email and we’ll send a secure sign-in link. No password to remember.

Button: **Email me a sign-in link**

Success message: **Check your email. Your secure MEANWHILE link is on the way.**

### Optional newsletter checkbox

`□ Send me an occasional MEANWHILE email with good things worth knowing.`

Supporting line: **Optional. Creating an account does not subscribe you. You
can unsubscribe at any time.**

Do not pre-check the box.

## Suggested confirmation-return page

**You’re in. Welcome to your MEANWHILE.**

Your email is confirmed and your edition can now follow you between devices.

Primary button: **Return to my edition**  
Secondary link: **Choose email preferences**

## Suggested email-preferences page

**Email Preferences**

- Account and security messages — required while the account exists
- Occasional MEANWHILE edition — optional
- Product updates and reader surveys — optional

Buttons: **Save choices** · **Unsubscribe from all optional email**

Account link: **Delete my account and synced information**

## Suggested account-deletion flow

**Delete your MEANWHILE account?**

This will remove your account and cloud-synced edition preferences, saved
stories, feedback and reading history according to our deletion schedule.
Information stored only in this browser may need to be cleared separately.
This cannot be undone.

Buttons: **Keep my account** · **Delete my account**

After deletion: **Your account-deletion request has been received. We’ll email
you when it is complete.**

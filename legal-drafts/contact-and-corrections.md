# Contact MEANWHILE

something is wrong, stale, repeated, overly political, depressing, poorly
credited or simply not in the spirit of the place, please tell us.

## Corrections and editorial concerns

Please include:

- what appears to be incorrect or inappropriate; and
- any supporting information that may help us review it.

MEANWHILE does not alter the original publisher’s page.

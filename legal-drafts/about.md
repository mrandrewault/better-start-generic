# About MEANWHILE

## The short version

MEANWHILE is a calmer way to discover what people are making, restoring,
learning, sharing and doing for one another.

The internet is full of fascinating work and generous human stories, but much
of it is buried beneath breaking-news alerts, outrage and the same handful of
large publishers. MEANWHILE looks elsewhere. We gather stories from independent,
local, specialist and culturally curious publications around the world, then
shape them into a lively visual edition.

There is no endless emergency here. That is the point.

## What you’ll find

Depending on the day, MEANWHILE might lead you to a neighborhood project, a
beautifully repaired machine, an artist’s studio, an animal finding a second
chance, a restaurant worth knowing, a scientific discovery, a memorable trip,
or a musician making something entirely new.

The mix is deliberately broad. We value human interest, community, animals,
music, art, food, design, books, travel, nature, invention, history and useful
curiosity. Sports belong when the story is really about people, culture,
craft, perseverance or joy—not scores, trades or roster chatter.

## What you won’t find

MEANWHILE is designed to be politics-free and rage-bait-free. We filter out
partisan coverage, elected officials, government drama, tragedy-first framing,
routine professional-sports news and the familiar technology-billionaire
content cycle.

No system is perfect. If something slips through, readers can flag it and help
us make the edition better.

## How it works

MEANWHILE does not republish a conventional newspaper. It discovers and
organizes links to work published by others. Every story card identifies its
source and sends the reader to the original publisher.

Our editorial software looks for freshness, variety, strong imagery and a
genuinely interesting reason to read. It also works to prevent the same story
from appearing twice and remembers what a reader has already seen.

Readers can use the general edition or make their own. Personalization changes
the emphasis—not the size of the world. Even a tailored edition retains room
for surprise.

## Independent publishers matter

At least 90% of the MEANWHILE source pool is intended to come from independent,
local, nonprofit or specialist publishers. They find stories that broad news
feeds routinely miss. MEANWHILE exists partly to help more people discover
their work and visit them directly.

## Who we are

MEANWHILE is a product of Ault Industries, based just outside New York City.
This is an independent experiment in adding a little more humanity, curiosity,
good times and random tangents to the daily mix of life. We’re also friends to
all animals.
